Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2026

TurnTap (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use the TurnTap mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

TurnTap is designed to work offline-first and does not collect personal data on servers.

The app may store the following information locally on your device only:

  • Building name (entered by the user)
  • Tenant / family names
  • Floor or room details
  • App preferences and rotation order

2. Permissions Used

TurnTap may request the following permission:

  • Notifications (optional): Used only to remind users about water timing or turn alerts, if enabled by the user.

The app does not access sensitive permissions such as contacts, camera, microphone, or location without user consent.

3. How We Use Information

The data you enter is used only to:

  • Manage water rotation schedules
  • Display tenant turns
  • Show reminders and alerts
  • Improve in-app user experience

All data remains on your device.

4. Data Storage & Security

All information is stored locally on your device. We do not upload, sync, or back up data to any external servers.

TurnTap does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

5. Data Sharing

TurnTap does not share your data with third parties, advertisers, analytics providers, or other users.

6. Children’s Privacy

TurnTap is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. User Control

You have full control over your data. You can edit or delete tenant information at any time. Uninstalling the app will remove all locally stored data.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
